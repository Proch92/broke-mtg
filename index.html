BROKE

A new budget-friendly Magic: The Gathering format

About the Format

Are you tired of having to sell a kidney just to play competitive MTG? Disappointed by the ridiculously high costs of supposedly budget-friendly formats like Pauper?

Then give Broke a try!

Broke is a new eternal format featuring only Commons and Uncommons from Foundations onwards.

As of now, there is no banlist, although changes may be introduced depending on the metagame and card prices.

Our top priority is to keep Broke affordable: individual cards should never cost more than a few euros.

The metagame is interesting, diverse, and far from trivial.
